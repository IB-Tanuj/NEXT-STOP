# Major Regions Where FlixBus Does Not Operate:
1. The Entire North-East: States like Assam (Guwahati), Meghalaya (Shillong), and Sikkim (Gangtok) are completely untouched by their network.
2. Eastern India: Major hubs like Kolkata, Patna (Bihar), and Bhubaneswar (Odisha) currently have no FlixBus coverage.
3. Deep Island Territories: Obviously, island destinations like the Andaman and Nicobar Islands or Lakshadweep are inaccessible by bus networks.

# All type of seats
1. Classic: A standard window or aisle seat of your choice.
2. Semi-bed / Bed: Reclinable or fully flat sleeper beds, which are highly popular on overnight routes in regions like FlixBus India.
3. Extra / Single: Books your seat and leaves the adjacent seat empty for maximum privacy.
4. Panorama: Seats in the very first row with an unobstructed front view.

Seating Configurations (India):
A/C Sleeper (2+1 layout): Rows of single berths on one side, double berths on the other.
A/C Semi-Sleeper (2+2 layout): Reclining seats with calf support.
Hybrid Seater/Sleeper: Lower seats with an upper tier of sleeper berths.

# Key Database Entities (Data Model)
To handle the unique constraints of a bus network (like the regional networks and seat types):
Stations: station_id, name, city, geo_coordinates, region_id.
Routes: route_id, origin_station_id, destination_station_id, total_distance_km.
Schedules / Trips: trip_id, route_id, bus_id, departure_time, arrival_time, base_price.
Buses: bus_id, operator_name, bus_type (e.g., Sleeper, Semi-Sleeper, Double-Decker), total_capacity.
Seat Configurations: seat_id, bus_id, seat_number, seat_category (e.g., Single-Bed, Panorama, Classic), price_multiplier.

#The Gender Allocation Rules (The Logic Grid)
The algorithmic rules governing the seating layout depend on the status of the booking object:
Rule A (Group/Couple Booking): If a male and a female passenger are wrapped in the same Booking ID (PNR), they can select and lock a double sleeper berth together. The system recognizes them as a traveling unit and permits the mixed-gender adjacent seating.
Rule B (Solo Female Booking): If a woman books a single seat within an empty double berth, the inventory engine immediately flags the adjacent seat as "Female Only".
Rule C (Solo Male Booking Attempt): If a solo male traveler later tries to buy a ticket, the system will block him from selecting that specific adjacent seat to ensure safety. He can only choose completely empty rows or seats next to other men.
