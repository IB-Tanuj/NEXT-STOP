# Major Regions Where FlixBus Does Not Operate:
1. The Entire North-East: States like Assam (Guwahati), Meghalaya (Shillong), and Sikkim (Gangtok) are completely untouched by their network.
2. Eastern India: Major hubs like Kolkata, Patna (Bihar), and Bhubaneswar (Odisha) currently have no FlixBus coverage.
3. Deep Island Territories: Obviously, island destinations like the Andaman and Nicobar Islands or Lakshadweep are inaccessible by bus networks.

# All type of seats

2 .Extra / Single: Books your seat and leaves the adjacent seat empty for maximum privacy.
3. Panorama: Seats in the very first row with an unobstructed front view.
